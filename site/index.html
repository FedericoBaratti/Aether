A music player for Windows, written in Rust.

Aether plays the audio files already present on the disk. Starting from a playlist — exported from a Spotify archive, read from an M3U file or given as a link — it compares the tracks against the local library and retrieves the missing ones from the free-access catalogues: Internet Archive and Audius. Every track acquired keeps the licence of the source it came from. Tracks unavailable in any free catalogue are collected in a purchase list, with links to the shops that pay the authors.

Audius: three distinct permissions

On Audius the permission is determined by the artist for each individual track. There are three distinct controls: the download switch and two gates, which can make access conditional on following the artist or on holding a token. Aether checks all three and considers permission granted only in the absence of prohibitions.

Jamendo: implemented, disabled

The module is implemented and covered by tests, but disabled in this version. The cause is not technical: the API is free for non-commercial uses only, and the terms define commercial use as «any monetary compensation». The module is already arranged for streaming playback alone — the terms prohibit caching and offline access — through aether-net::FlussoHttp, which plays a track without writing it to disk.

What no free source covers

No free-access source distributes the commercial catalogue. This is not a technical limit that can be worked around: it is the reason other programs carry out operations not permitted by the terms of the services they use. Aether covers what it can through lawful channels and, for the rest of the catalogue, indicates where to buy it.

04 — Personalisation

A skin redefines 52 components. It is not an alternation between a light theme and a dark one.

A skin is a validated data document, not a stylesheet: 47 tokens, 52 named parts and 11 effects with a declared cost. The program parses it, reports its warnings and compiles it to deterministic CSS.

Version 2.2.0, for Windows, unsigned.

The installer for Windows is available among the assets of the latest release: an NSIS executable. It is not signed with a code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen blocks its execution on first launch: «More info», then «Run anyway». The reasons for the absence of the signature are stated among the known limits.

Reports and proposals

The program is released and still under development. Reports of failures and proposals for what it should do next are received at the same address. The procedure for reporting vulnerabilities is in SECURITY.md.

barattifederico@gmail.com
Free, and it stays free

There is no paid version and none is planned: two of the integrated sources, the Live Music Archive and Jamendo, are subject to non-commercial clauses that a paid version would violate. The constraint is stated explicitly in the terms of use.
